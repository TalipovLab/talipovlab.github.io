Before you apply

  • Check the specific citizenship or residency, enrollment, academic-standing, and other eligibility requirements. Do not assume all programs use the same rules.
  • Record the deadline and time zone, recommendation deadlines, dates of participation, and required materials.
  • Compare the project and mentoring arrangements, compensation, travel and housing support, and any costs you would need to cover.
  • Tailor your statement to the scientific work. Explain what you want to learn and how your experience prepares you to begin.
  • Confirm credit or scheduling arrangements with your academic adviser when relevant.

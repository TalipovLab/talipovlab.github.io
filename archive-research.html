Research Interests

The research activities in our lab focus on achieving a detailed understanding of the molecular electronic structures, excited states, reaction intermediates, and transition states of various chemical reactions for the design of better catalysts, materials, and drugs.

Advanced electronic structure calculations and theoretical modeling

  • Discovery of Novel Small Molecules with Unique Electronic Structures and Chemical Bonding
  • Design of Molecules for Novel Photovoltaic Materials

Molecular dynamics simulations

  • Rational Drug Design by  Modeling the Protein-Drug Interactions
  • Evaluation of Viscoelastic Properties of Cells from the Molecular Dynamics Simulations

Applications of machine learning in chemistry

  • Machine learning for rapid and accurate ligand-protein docking

Currently inactive

  • Computational Design of Nanoparticle Catalysts for In-Situ Upgrade of Heavy Oil Fractions

Principal investigator

Marat R. Talipov

Associate Professor · Physical Chemistry

Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry
New Mexico State University

Marat joined NMSU in 2016 and became Associate Professor in 2022. His research connects chemical bonding and electronic structure with molecular reactivity and function, from reactive oxygen and nitrogen species to conjugated materials, enzyme inhibitors, and lipid membranes.

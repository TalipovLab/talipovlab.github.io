Research opportunities

Interested in the molecular foundations of chemistry? Get in touch to discuss research, graduate study, or collaboration.

Graduate students

Develop your research direction.

Our work connects chemical theory, molecular simulation, and scientific software. If these interests align with yours, email Marat with a brief introduction, your research interests, and your academic background.

We can discuss potential projects and how they fit your training goals. Formal admission information is available through the department.

Undergraduate researchers

Interested in trying computational research? Tell us what you are studying, which parts of chemistry interest you, and what you would like to learn. We can discuss suitable ways to get involved.

Talipov Lab

Our lab focuses on quantum chemistry calculations, molecular dynamics modeling, and protein-ligand interaction studies. Our aim is to advance the creation of future catalysts, materials, and pharmaceuticals.

If you are interested in doing research in our group:

  • PhD/Master Applicatnts: Reach out toDr. Talipov
  • Undergraduate Students: Connect with Dr. Talipov or any team member. Also, explore these NMSU resources.
  • High/Middle School Students: Need a mentor for your science fair endeavor? Get in touch with Dr. Talipov

Feel free to reach out if you have any questions or are interested in collaborating with us.
